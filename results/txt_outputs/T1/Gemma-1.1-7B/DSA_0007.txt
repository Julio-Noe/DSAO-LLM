Here is the Executive Summary for the Data Sharing Agreement (DSA):

This Data Sharing Agreement (DSA) between OrgA and OrgD aims to detect fraud and perform analytics on specific data categories, including biometric and financial information. The agreement is governed by a purpose-specific governance plan, incident response plan, and exit plan. OrgA acts as the data controller, while OrgD serves as the processor.

The DSA purposes are fraud detection and analytics, with legal bases of contract and legitimate interest. The data categories used or shared include biometric and financial information.

The dataset, DSA_0007_DS1, conforms to a specific standard and is stored for 18 months with an anonymization disposal method. Rights handling procedures are in place, including responsible party OrgA and a procedure URL.

Transparency arrangements include notice types and issuers. An assessment has been conducted, concluding that the DSA poses a high risk.

The governance plan includes review frequencies and audit rights. Incident response procedures include notification deadlines and contact points. The exit plan outlines triggers, data disposition, and post-termination obligations.

This DSA is applicable to a specific population of employees.
