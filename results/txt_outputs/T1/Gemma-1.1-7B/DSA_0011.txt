Here is the Executive Summary for the Data Sharing Agreement (DSA):

This Data Sharing Agreement (DSA) between OrgD and OrgB outlines the terms for sharing data. The parties, OrgD (Data Controller) and OrgB (Processor), will use the shared data for reporting and analytics purposes, with legal bases grounded in public interest. The data categories used include location and contact information. The dataset, DSA_0011_DS1, will be stored for 18 months and disposed of through return. Rights handling is managed by OrgD, responsible for rectification rights, with a procedure URL provided. Transparency arrangements include direct notice from OrgD and an audit right for on-site annual reviews. Governance, incident response, and exit plans are also outlined, including review frequencies, metrics, and obligations.
