Here is the executive summary of the Data Sharing Agreement (DSA):

This DSA, version 1.0.5, was created on November 14, 2025. The agreement involves two parties: OrgA, acting as the Data Controller, and OrgE, acting as the Recipient. The purposes of this DSA are analytics and fraud detection. The legal bases for processing are legitimate interest and public interest.

The DSA uses biometric, financial, and contact data categories. The dataset is stored for 12 months and will be disposed of using the "SecureDelete" method. The rights handling procedure is responsible for rectification, and the responsible party is OrgA.

The transparency arrangement includes a notice type of "layered" and an issuer of OrgA. The governance plan is reviewed every 12 months, with a breach metric of "BreachMTTR<=72h" and an audit right for remote audits. The incident response plan includes a notification deadline of 72 hours, and the contact point is "mailto:sec@example.org".

The exit plan includes an exit trigger of "contract_end", a data disposition method of "SecureDelete", and a post-termination obligation of "no_contact_after_exit". The population covered by this DSA is general.
