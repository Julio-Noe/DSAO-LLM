Here is the executive summary of the Data Sharing Agreement (DSA):

This DSA, version 1.0.2, was created on November 14, 2025. The agreement involves two parties: OrgA, a processor, and OrgB, a data controller. The purposes of the DSA are research and analytics, with legal bases of contract and public interest. The data categories used or shared include biometric and financial information.

The DSA specifies that the dataset will be stored for six months, with a disposal method of return. The rights handling procedure is managed by OrgB, and the transparency arrangement includes a notice type of direct notification from OrgB.

The governance plan outlines review frequency, metrics, and audit rights. The incident response plan includes a notification deadline of 72 hours and a contact point for security incidents. The exit plan specifies an exit trigger, data disposition, and post-termination obligations.

Assessment information is available, including a TRA assessment outcome of high risk. International transfer information is not specified.

This DSA provides a framework for the sharing and processing of personal data between OrgA and OrgB, with clear roles, purposes, and procedures in place to ensure compliance with data protection regulations.
