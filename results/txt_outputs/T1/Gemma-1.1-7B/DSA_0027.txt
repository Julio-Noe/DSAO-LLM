Here is the executive summary of the Data Sharing Agreement (DSA):

This DSA, version 1.0.2, was created on November 14, 2025. The agreement involves two parties: OrgD, acting as the Data Controller, and OrgB, acting as the Recipient. The purposes of this DSA are analytics and reporting, with a legal basis of legitimate interest. The data categories involved include health and location information.

The DSA specifies that the data will be stored for six months, with a disposal method of SecureDelete. The agreement also includes provisions for international transfer to Singapore, with safeguards such as pseudonymization and access control.

The DSA includes arrangements for rights handling, including the right to rectification, and transparency obligations, such as layered notice and a procedure URL. The agreement also includes an assessment report, which identified a medium-level risk.

The governance plan outlines the review frequency and audit rights, while the incident response plan specifies a notification deadline and contact point. The exit plan outlines the trigger for exit, data disposition, and post-termination obligations.

Finally, the DSA includes enhanced safeguards for vulnerable populations, such as children, and age-appropriate design.
