Here is the executive summary of the Data Sharing Agreement (DSA):

This DSA, version 1.0.1, was created on November 14, 2025. The agreement involves two parties: OrgC, acting as the Data Controller, and OrgE, acting as the Recipient. The purposes of this DSA are research and service provision. The legal bases for processing are a legal obligation and legitimate interest.

The DSA uses data categories related to location and health. The dataset, DSA_0033_DS1, conforms to a specific standard and will be stored for six months before being disposed of through anonymization. The international transfer of data to South Africa will be done through a business continuity and recovery service (BCRs) with safeguards in place, including access control and pseudonymization.

The rights handling procedure is responsible for the right to erasure, with OrgC being the responsible party and providing a procedure URL. Transparency arrangements include direct notice to individuals, with OrgC as the issuer and a webpage providing information.

The governance plan includes regular reviews every six months, with metrics such as breach mean time to recovery (MTTR) and audit rights for remote audits. The incident response plan includes a notification deadline of 72 hours, with contact information provided. The exit plan outlines the procedures for exiting the agreement, including secure deletion of data and no redisclosure obligations.

An assessment has been conducted, classified as low-risk, with a DPIA (Data Protection Impact Assessment) report available.
