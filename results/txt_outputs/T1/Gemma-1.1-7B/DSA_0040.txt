Here is the executive summary:

This Data Sharing Agreement (DSA) between OrgA and OrgD aims to detect fraud and perform analytics. The parties have defined purposes for processing personal data, including location and biometric information. The agreement specifies that OrgA will act as the Data Controller, while OrgD is the Recipient. The data will be stored for 18 months and disposed of through a return method. Rights handling procedures have been established, including the right to erasure and a responsible party (OrgA). Transparency arrangements include direct notice from OrgA, with a review frequency of every 6 months. The agreement has undergone an assessment, which identified a medium-level risk and provided recommendations for improvement. Governance, incident response, and exit plans are also in place to ensure the secure sharing of data.
