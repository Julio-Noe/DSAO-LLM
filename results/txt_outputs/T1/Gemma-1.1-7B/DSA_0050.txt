Here is the executive summary of the Data Sharing Agreement (DSA):

This DSA, version 1.0.5, was created on November 14, 2025. The agreement involves two parties: OrgA, acting as the data controller, and OrgC, acting as the recipient. The purposes of this DSA are reporting and research, with legal bases established under contract law. The data categories involved include health and financial information.

The DSA specifies that the shared dataset, DSA_0050_DS1, will be stored for 12 months and disposed of through anonymization. The rights handling process, DSA_0050_RH1, ensures that the right to erasure is respected, with OrgA responsible for handling these rights and providing a procedure URL.

The transparency arrangement, DSA_0050_TA1, provides layered notices and is issued by OrgA. The assessment, DSA_0050_Assess, has been conducted and is available for review.

The governance plan, DSA_0050_Gov, outlines the review frequency and audit rights. The incident response plan, DSA_0050_IRP, specifies a notification deadline and contact point. The exit plan, DSA_0050_Exit, outlines the trigger for exit and data disposition.

The population covered by this DSA is children. The enhanced safeguard in place is age-appropriate design.
