Here is the executive summary of the Data Sharing Agreement (DSA):

This DSA, version 1.0.5, was created on November 14, 2025. The agreement involves two parties: OrgC, acting as the Data Controller, and OrgE, acting as the Recipient. The purposes of this DSA are fraud detection and service provision. The parties will process personal data, including location and health information, in accordance with the contract legal basis. The retention period for this data is 18 months, and disposal will be done by returning the data. The rights handling procedure includes the right to erasure, and the responsible party is OrgC. Transparency arrangements include indirect notice provision by OrgC. An assessment has been conducted, and the outcome is low risk. The governance plan includes regular reviews every 12 months, with a metric of audit findings less than or equal to 2. Incident response plans are in place, with a notification deadline of 72 hours and contact point for security incidents. The exit plan includes secure deletion of data upon contract end, with no redisclosure obligations after termination. Finally, the population covered by this DSA is children, and enhanced safeguards are in place to ensure age-appropriate design.
