Here is the executive summary of the Data Sharing Agreement (DSA):

This DSA, version 1.0.2, was created on November 14, 2025. The agreement involves two parties: OrgD, acting as the Data Controller, and OrgE, acting as the Processor. The purposes of this DSA are analytics and reporting. The legal bases for processing are a legal obligation and a contract.

The agreement uses financial, health, biometric data categories. The retention period is 12 months, and the disposal method is return of data to the controller.

The rights handling procedure includes the right to erasure, and the responsible party is OrgD. The transparency arrangement provides direct notice from OrgD.

The governance plan includes a review frequency of every 12 months, with metrics and audit rights. The incident response plan has a notification deadline of 72 hours, and the contact point is sec@example.org.

The exit plan includes an exit trigger of contract end, with data disposition and post-termination obligations. The population covered by this DSA is children, and the enhanced safeguard is age-appropriate design.

Assessment information is available, including a DPIA assessment outcome of "medium" and references to the assessment report.
