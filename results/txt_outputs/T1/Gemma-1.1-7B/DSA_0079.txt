Here is the Executive Summary for the Data Sharing Agreement (DSA):

This Data Sharing Agreement (DSA) between OrgB and OrgD aims to facilitate the sharing of data for fraud detection and reporting purposes. The parties have agreed on specific purposes, processing activities, and legal bases for the data sharing. OrgB will act as the Data Controller, while OrgD will be the Recipient of the shared data. The DSA specifies that financial and biometric data categories will be used for fraud detection, while location data will be used for reporting purposes. The shared dataset will be stored for 18 months and disposed of through a return method. The DSA also includes provisions for international transfer, with OrgB ensuring the safe transfer of data to Japan. The agreement outlines rights handling procedures for rectification, responsible party OrgB, and procedure URL. Transparency arrangements include notice types and issuers. The DSA has undergone an assessment, which identified a medium-level risk. Governance, incident response, and exit plans are also in place to ensure the secure sharing of data. Enhanced safeguards have been implemented for vulnerable populations, including children.
