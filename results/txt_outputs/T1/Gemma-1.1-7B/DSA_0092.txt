Here is the executive summary of the Data Sharing Agreement (DSA):

This DSA, version 1.0.3, was created on November 14, 2025. The agreement involves two parties: OrgB, acting as the Data Controller, and OrgE, acting as the Recipient. The purposes of this DSA are analytics and reporting. The legal bases for processing are a legal obligation and legitimate interest.

The agreement uses data categories related to health and location. The retention period is 12 months, with a disposal method of returning the data. Rights handling procedures are in place for erasure, and transparency arrangements include indirect notice and a procedure URL.

The governance plan includes regular reviews every 12 months, with audit rights for on-site annual audits. The incident response plan provides for notification within 72 hours, with a contact point for security incidents.

The exit plan outlines the procedures for exiting the agreement, including returning data and no contact after termination. The population covered by this DSA is general.

Assessment information is available, including a DPIA assessment outcome of "high" and references to the assessment report.
