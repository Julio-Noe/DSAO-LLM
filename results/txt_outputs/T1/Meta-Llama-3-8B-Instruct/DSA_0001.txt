Here is the Executive Summary:

This Data Sharing Agreement (DSA) between OrgA and OrgE outlines the terms for sharing personal data. The parties' roles are clearly defined, with OrgA acting as the Data Controller and OrgE as the Recipient. The purposes of data sharing are analytics and fraud detection, with legal bases established under contract and public interest. The agreement specifies the data categories used, including biometric and contact information, and outlines retention periods of 12 months. The parties have implemented governance, incident response, and exit plans to ensure compliance with data protection regulations. Additionally, the agreement includes assessment information, international transfer details, and enhanced safeguards for vulnerable populations.
