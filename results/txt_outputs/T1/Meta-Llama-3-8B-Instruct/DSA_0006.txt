Here is the executive summary of the Data Sharing Agreement (DSA):

This DSA, version 1.0.3, was created on November 14, 2025. The agreement involves two parties: OrgB, acting as the data controller, and OrgD, acting as the processor. The purposes of this DSA are reporting and analytics, with legal bases of public interest and legal obligation. The data categories used or shared include biometric and financial information.

The dataset, DSA_0006_DS1, conforms to a standard and will be stored for 12 months before being disposed of securely. The rights handling, DSA_0006_RH1, is responsible for rectification and has a procedure in place. Transparency arrangements, DSA_0006_TA1, will provide direct notices to individuals.

The governance plan, DSA_0006_Gov, includes regular reviews and remote audits. The incident response plan, DSA_0006_IRP, has a notification deadline of 72 hours and a contact point for security incidents. The exit plan, DSA_0006_Exit, outlines the procedures for returning data and no redisclosure after termination.

The population covered by this DSA is children, and enhanced safeguards are in place to ensure age-appropriate design. An assessment, DSA_0006_Assess, has been conducted and is available for review.
