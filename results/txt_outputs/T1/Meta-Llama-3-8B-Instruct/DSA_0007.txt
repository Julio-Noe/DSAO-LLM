Here is the executive summary for the Data Sharing Agreement (DSA):

This DSA, version 1.0.0, was created on November 14, 2025. The agreement involves two parties: OrgA, a data controller with the role of "Data Controller", and OrgD, a processor with the role of "Processor". The purposes of this DSA are fraud detection and analytics, with legal bases being a contract and legitimate interest. The data categories used or shared include biometric, financial, and location information.

The dataset is stored for 18 months and will be anonymized upon disposal. The rights handling involves access rights managed by OrgA, with a responsible party and procedure URL provided. Transparency arrangements include layered notices issued by OrgA, with a notice type of "layered" and an issuer URL provided.

Assessment information is available, with a DPIA assessment outcome of "high" and references to a PDF document. Governance plans include review frequencies, metrics, and audit rights, while incident response plans outline notification deadlines and contact points. Exit obligations include data disposition and post-termination obligations.

This DSA does not involve international transfers or enhanced safeguards for child or vulnerable populations.
