Here is the executive summary of the Data Sharing Agreement (DSA):

This DSA, version 1.0.1, was created on November 14, 2025. The agreement involves two parties: OrgA, acting as the Data Controller, and OrgE, acting as the Recipient. The purposes of this DSA are reporting and service provision, with a legal basis of legitimate interest. The data categories used and shared include contact information and biometric data.

The dataset, DSA_0009_DS1, conforms to a standard and will be stored for 12 months before being disposed of through anonymization. The rights handling, DSA_0009_RH1, is managed by OrgA, and the transparency arrangement, DSA_0009_TA1, requires direct notice from OrgA.

The governance plan, DSA_0009_Gov, includes regular reviews every 12 months and remote audits. The incident response plan, DSA_0009_IRP, requires notification within 72 hours and contact through a specific email address. The exit plan, DSA_0009_Exit, includes secure deletion of data and no contact after the exit.

The population covered by this DSA is general, and an assessment has been conducted, with a low risk outcome. The assessment report can be found at <https://example.org/dpia.pdf>.
