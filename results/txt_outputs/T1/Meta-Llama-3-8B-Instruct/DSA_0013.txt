Here is the executive summary of the Data Sharing Agreement (DSA):

This DSA, version 1.0.0, was created on November 14, 2025. The agreement involves two parties: OrgA, acting as the Data Controller, and OrgB, acting as the Recipient. The purposes of this DSA are analytics and reporting. The parties will process personal data, including location and biometric information, under the legal bases of public interest and contract. The retention period for this data is 12 months, with disposal method "Anonymise". Rights handling procedures are in place for rectification, and transparency arrangements include layered notices issued by OrgA. The DSA has undergone a Data Protection Impact Assessment (DPIA) with an outcome of "high" risk. The governance plan includes regular reviews, breach metrics, and on-site audits. Incident response procedures are in place, including notification deadlines and contact points. The exit plan outlines the trigger for exit, data disposition, and post-termination obligations. Finally, enhanced safeguards are in place to protect children's personal data, including age-appropriate design.
