Here is the executive summary of the Data Sharing Agreement (DSA):

This DSA, version 1.0.1, was created on November 14, 2025. The agreement involves two parties: OrgB, acting as the Data Controller, and OrgE, acting as the Recipient. The purposes of this DSA are research and analytics, with legal bases including a legal obligation and legitimate interest. The data categories used or shared include financial and location information.

The DSA specifies a retention period of 18 months, with data to be disposed of securely. Rights handling is managed by OrgB, which is responsible for access rights and has a procedure in place. Transparency arrangements include indirect notice and an issuer, OrgB.

The governance plan outlines review frequency, metrics, and audit rights. The incident response plan includes notification deadlines and contact points. The exit plan specifies triggers, data disposition, and post-termination obligations.

The DSA also includes population information, with enhanced safeguards in place for children. Assessment information is not specified.
