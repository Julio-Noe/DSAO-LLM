Here is the Executive Summary for the Data Sharing Agreement (DSA):

This Data Sharing Agreement (DSA) between OrgA and OrgD outlines the terms for sharing data between the two parties. The primary purposes of this DSA are reporting and research, with legal bases established under public interest and legal obligation. The parties will process personal data categories including contact information and location, with storage and retention periods of 12 months. The DSA includes provisions for international data transfer to South Africa, with safeguards in place to protect the transferred data. The agreement also outlines rights handling procedures, including access and responsible party OrgA, as well as transparency arrangements, including notice types and issuers. The DSA has undergone an assessment, which identified a high risk level, and includes governance, incident response, and exit plans to ensure compliance with data protection regulations.
