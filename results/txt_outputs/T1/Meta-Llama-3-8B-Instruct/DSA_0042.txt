Here is the Executive Summary:

This Data Sharing Agreement (DSA) between OrgA and OrgB outlines the terms for sharing data. The parties involved are OrgA, a recipient, and OrgB, a data controller. The purposes of the DSA are reporting and service provision, with legal bases established through contracts. Data categories used or shared include financial and location information for reporting purposes, and biometric data for service provision. The dataset is stored for 18 months and will be disposed of through return to the data subject. Rights handling procedures are in place for erasure, with OrgB responsible and a procedure URL provided. Transparency arrangements include indirect notice provision by OrgB. Governance, incident response, and exit plans are also outlined, including review frequencies, metrics, and audit rights. Enhanced safeguards are in place to protect children's data, with age-appropriate design considerations.
