Here is the executive summary of the Data Sharing Agreement (DSA):

This DSA, version 1.0.1, was created on November 14, 2025. The agreement involves two parties: OrgD, acting as the Data Controller, and OrgC, acting as the Processor. The purposes of this DSA are service provision and research, with legal bases including a legal obligation and public interest. The data categories involved are health and location.

The DSA specifies that the dataset will be stored for 18 months, with a disposal method of returning the data. The rights handling procedure is responsible to OrgD and includes procedures for erasure.

The transparency arrangement requires direct notice from the Data Controller, OrgD. The DSA has undergone an assessment, which concluded that there is a medium-level risk of data breaches. The governance plan includes regular reviews every 6 months, with metrics for breach response time and audit rights. The incident response plan requires notification within 72 hours, with a contact point for security incidents.

The exit plan specifies that data will be returned to the Data Controller upon contract end, with no obligation for further disclosure. The population covered by this DSA is employees.

Note that the assessment information, international transfer information, and child or vulnerable-population safeguards are not specified in this DSA.
