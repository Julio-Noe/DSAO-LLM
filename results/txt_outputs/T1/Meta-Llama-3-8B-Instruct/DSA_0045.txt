Here is the executive summary of the Data Sharing Agreement (DSA):

This DSA, version 1.0.3, was created on November 14, 2025. The agreement involves two parties: OrgD, acting as the Data Controller, and OrgB, acting as the Recipient. The purposes of this DSA are research and reporting, with legal bases including a legal obligation and public interest. The data categories involved are health, location, financial, and other unspecified categories.

The DSA specifies that the data will be stored for six months and disposed of through a return process. International transfer of data is planned to Germany, with encryption at rest and pseudonymization as safeguards.

The agreement includes arrangements for rights handling, transparency, and incident response. The Data Controller is responsible for erasure of personal data, and the procedure for this is available at a specified URL. Transparency arrangements include direct notice to individuals.

An assessment has been conducted, which identified a high risk and provided recommendations. The DSA includes governance, incident response, and exit plans, with review frequencies of six months and audit rights for third-party auditors. The Data Controller has implemented enhanced safeguards, including age-appropriate design, to protect children's data.
