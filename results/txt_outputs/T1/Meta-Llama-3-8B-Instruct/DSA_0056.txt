Here is the executive summary of the Data Sharing Agreement (DSA):

This DSA, version 1.0.4, was created on November 14, 2025. The agreement involves two parties: OrgC, acting as the Data Controller, and OrgE, acting as the Processor. The purposes of this DSA are service provision and research, with a legal basis of legitimate interest. The parties will process personal data categories including location and biometric information.

The DSA specifies that the processed data will be stored for six months, with a disposal method of return. The rights handling procedure includes the right to erasure, and the responsible party is OrgC.

The transparency arrangement requires indirect notice, with issuer OrgC and a procedure URL. The DSA has undergone an assessment, which identified a low risk level.

The governance plan includes regular reviews every six months, with audit rights for remote audits. The incident response plan requires notification within 72 hours, and the contact point is sec@example.org.

The exit plan specifies that data will be disposed of securely upon contract end, with no obligation to disclose the data further. The population covered by this DSA is employees.

This summary provides a concise overview of the key elements of the Data Sharing Agreement, including parties, purposes, data categories, retention, rights, governance, incident response, and exit obligations.
