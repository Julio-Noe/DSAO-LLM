Here is the executive summary of the Data Sharing Agreement (DSA):

This DSA, version 1.0.3, was created on November 14, 2025. The agreement involves two parties: OrgE, acting as the Data Controller, and OrgA, acting as the Recipient. The purposes of this DSA are fraud detection and analytics. The legal bases for processing personal data are a legal obligation and legitimate interest.

The DSA uses two categories of personal data: biometric and financial. The retention period for this data is 12 months, with a disposal method of secure deletion. Rights handling procedures are in place for the right to erasure, with OrgE as the responsible party and a procedure URL provided.

The DSA has an assessment outcome of "high" risk, as determined by a Data Protection Impact Assessment (DPIA). The governance plan includes regular reviews every 6 months, with a metric for measuring compliance and on-site audits conducted annually. Incident response procedures are in place, including notification deadlines and contact points.

The exit plan outlines the process for returning data to OrgE when the agreement ends, with a post-termination obligation not to contact individuals after exit. The population covered by this DSA is employees.
