Here is the executive summary of the Data Sharing Agreement (DSA):

This DSA, version 1.0.2, was created on November 14, 2025. The agreement involves two parties: OrgB, acting as the Data Controller, and OrgD, acting as the Processor. The purposes of this DSA are research and reporting, with a legal basis of public interest. The data categories involved include health, biometric, and contact information.

The DSA specifies that the dataset will be stored for 18 months and disposed of through a return method. The rights handling procedure is responsible to OrgB, which will erase personal data upon request.

The transparency arrangement includes a notice type of layered and an issuer of OrgB, with a procedure URL provided. The assessment outcome is medium, and the DPIA report is available at https://example.org/dpia.pdf.

The governance plan includes a review frequency of every 6 months, with an audit right to conduct on-site audits annually. The incident response plan specifies a notification deadline of 72 hours, with a contact point provided.

The exit plan outlines the trigger for exit as contract end, with data disposition through secure deletion and post-termination obligations to have no contact after exit. The population involved is children, with enhanced safeguards in place for age-appropriate design.

This DSA has undergone a review note, which identified the need to reduce data categories and provided guidance on how to achieve this.
