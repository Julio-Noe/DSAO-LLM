Here is the Executive Summary for the Data Sharing Agreement (DSA) in clear English text:

This Data Sharing Agreement (DSA) between OrgD and OrgC aims to facilitate the sharing of data for research and service provision purposes. The parties have agreed on specific processing activities, including the use of data categories such as location and health. The DSA has a governance plan in place, which includes regular reviews and audits to ensure compliance with the agreement. In case of an incident, OrgD will notify OrgC within 72 hours and provide a contact point for further communication. The agreement also includes an exit plan, which outlines the procedures for terminating the data sharing arrangement and ensuring the secure deletion of data. Additionally, OrgD has implemented enhanced safeguards to protect children's personal data, including age-appropriate design. The DSA has undergone an assessment, which identified areas for improvement and recommended changes to reduce data categories.
