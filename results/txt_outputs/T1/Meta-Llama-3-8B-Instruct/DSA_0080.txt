Here is the Executive Summary:

This Data Sharing Agreement (DSA) between OrgD and OrgC outlines the terms for sharing data between the two parties. The primary purposes of this DSA are reporting and research, with legal bases established under the legal obligation and public interest. The parties will process personal data categories including location and health, with storage and retention periods of 18 months. The DSA includes provisions for incident response, governance, and exit obligations, including a review frequency of every 6 months and an audit right for third-party auditors. Additionally, the parties have implemented enhanced safeguards to protect children's data, including age-appropriate design and secure deletion of personal data upon exit. The DSA has undergone an assessment, which concluded that the risks are low and provided a detailed report.
