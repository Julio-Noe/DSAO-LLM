Here is the Executive Summary for the Data Sharing Agreement (DSA):

This Data Sharing Agreement (DSA) between OrgB and OrgE enables the sharing of data for analytics and reporting purposes. The agreement's primary purpose is to support OrgB's role as a data controller, while OrgE acts as the recipient of the shared data. The DSA covers two processing activities: one for analytics and another for reporting, both of which use location and health data categories. The agreement specifies that the shared dataset will be stored for 12 months before being disposed of through return. The rights handling process is managed by OrgB, which is responsible for erasure requests and has a procedure in place for handling such requests. Transparency arrangements include indirect notice provision by OrgB, and the agreement has undergone a Data Protection Impact Assessment (DPIA) with an outcome of "high". The governance plan outlines review frequencies, metrics, and audit rights, while the incident response plan specifies notification deadlines and contact points. Finally, the exit plan outlines triggers, data disposition, and post-termination obligations.
