Here is the Executive Summary for the Data Sharing Agreement (DSA) in clear English text:

This Data Sharing Agreement (DSA) between OrgB and OrgD aims to facilitate the sharing of data for fraud detection and research purposes. The parties have designated roles, with OrgB acting as the Data Controller and OrgD as the Processor. The DSA has been assessed to have a high risk level, with measures in place to mitigate this risk. The data categories involved include contact and biometric information for fraud detection, and health-related data for research purposes. The data will be stored for six months and then anonymized, in accordance with the DSA's disposal method. The parties have also agreed on a governance plan, incident response plan, and exit plan to ensure the secure handling of personal data. Transparency arrangements are in place, including a notice type and issuer, to inform individuals about the processing of their personal data.
