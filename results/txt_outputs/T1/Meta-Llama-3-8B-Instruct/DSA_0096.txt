Here is the executive summary of the Data Sharing Agreement (DSA):

This DSA, version 1.0.1, was created on November 14, 2025. The agreement involves two parties: OrgC, acting as the Data Controller, and OrgA, acting as the Processor. The purposes of this DSA are research and service provision, with a legal basis of legitimate interest. The data categories involved include biometric and health information.

The DSA specifies that the data will be stored for 18 months, with a disposal method of SecureDelete. The rights handling involves access to the data by OrgC, and the responsible party is also OrgC. The transparency arrangement includes a notice type of indirect and an issuer of OrgC.

The DSA has undergone an assessment, which resulted in a high outcome. The governance plan includes review frequency of every 12 months and audit rights on site annually. The incident response plan specifies a notification deadline of 72 hours, and the contact point is sec@example.org. The exit plan includes an exit trigger of contract end, data disposition of SecureDelete, and post-termination obligation of no redisclosure.

The DSA population is children, and an enhanced safeguard has been implemented to ensure age-appropriate design.
