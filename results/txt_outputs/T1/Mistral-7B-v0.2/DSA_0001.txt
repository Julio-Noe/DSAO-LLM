The DSA is a contract between OrgA and OrgE. It covers two processing activities: analytics and fraud detection.
The analytics activity uses biometric and contact data, while the fraud detection activity uses only biometric data.
The analytics activity is based on a contractual agreement, while the fraud detection activity is based on public interest.
The data is stored for 12 months and disposed of securely.
The data is transferred internationally to Germany using SCCs, and the transfer is safeguarded by encryption at rest and access control.
The rights handling procedure for access to the data is managed by OrgA, and the transparency arrangement is a layered notice issued by OrgA.
The DSA has been assessed using TRA and found to have a low risk level.
The governance plan includes reviews every 12 months, with metrics such as BreachMTTR and remote audit rights.
The incident response plan includes a notification deadline of 72 hours and contact information for security@example.org.
The exit plan includes data disposal, no redisclosure, and contract end as triggers.
The population covered by the DSA is employees.
