The DSA is a Data Sharing Agreement between two organizations, OrgD and OrgC.
OrgD is the Data Controller and OrgC is the Recipient.
The DSA has two purposes: research and reporting.
OrgD is responsible for handling rights requests related to data erasure, while OrgC is responsible for handling rights requests related to access.
The DSA includes a governance plan, an incident response plan, and an exit plan.
The governance plan includes a review frequency of every 6 months, a metric for audit findings, and an audit right of third-party audits.
The incident response plan includes a notification deadline of 72 hours and a contact point for security incidents.
The exit plan includes an exit trigger of contract end, data disposition of secure delete, and a post-termination obligation of no contact after exit.
The DSA also includes a population of children, and enhanced safeguards for age-appropriate design.
The DSA includes a review note that justifies the processing activity of research and recommends reducing data categories.
The DSA includes an international transfer to Japan, with safeguards of encryption at rest and access control.
The DSA includes a rights handling for data erasure, with responsible party of OrgD and procedure URL.
The DSA includes a transparency arrangement for indirect notice, with issuer of OrgD and page URL.
The DSA includes an assessment for TRA, with outcome of medium and references to a DPIA.
