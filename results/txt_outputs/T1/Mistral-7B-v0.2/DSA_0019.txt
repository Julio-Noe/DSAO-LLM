The Data Sharing Agreement (DSA) is an agreement between two parties, OrgE and OrgC. The DSA has two purposes: fraud detection and research.

OrgE is the data controller, and OrgC is the recipient of the data. The DSA specifies two processing activities: fraud detection and research. Both activities are subject to legal obligations, and the data categories used in each activity are financial, health, and biometric.

The DSA specifies a shared dataset, which conforms to a standard and has storage and retention periods of 6 months. The dataset is disposed of by anonymization.

The DSA specifies a rights handling procedure for the right to erasure, which is handled by OrgE. The DSA also specifies a transparency arrangement with indirect notice and an issuer of the notice.

The DSA specifies an assessment that was conducted, which resulted in a high-risk finding. The DSA also specifies a governance plan, an incident response plan, and an exit plan. The governance plan specifies a review frequency of every 6 months, a metric for breach mean time to repair (MTTR) of less than 72 hours, and an audit right of on-site audits every year. The incident response plan specifies a notification deadline of 72 hours and a contact point for security incidents. The exit plan specifies an exit trigger of contract end, data disposal by secure delete, and a post-termination obligation of no contact after exit. The DSA also specifies a population of employees.

The DSA is subject to international transfer, and the destination country is specified. The transfer mechanism is also specified, as well as any safeguards in place for the transfer.

The DSA also specifies enhanced safeguards for child or vulnerable populations.
