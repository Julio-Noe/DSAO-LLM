```
This Data Sharing Agreement (DSA) governs the sharing of data between OrgB and OrgD.

OrgB is a Data Controller, responsible for collecting and processing personal data. OrgD is a Processor, responsible for analyzing the data on behalf of OrgB.

The purposes of this DSA are fraud detection and research. The data categories used/shared include contact information, health records, and financial data.

The dataset is stored for 12 months and then securely deleted. The disposal method is SecureDelete.

The DSA includes an international transfer to ZA, with Adequacy as the transfer mechanism and key separation and pseudonymization as safeguards.

The rights handling includes a procedure for erasure, with OrgB as the responsible party. The transparency arrangement is indirect, with OrgB as the issuer and a webpage as the notice.

The governance plan includes an audit every 12 months, with a metric of AuditFindings<=2 and on-site audits yearly.

The incident response plan includes a notification deadline of 72 hours and contact information for OrgB.

The exit plan includes a trigger of contract end, data disposition of return, and post-termination obligation of no redisclosure.

The population is employees.
```
