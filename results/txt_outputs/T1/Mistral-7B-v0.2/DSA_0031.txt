The DSA is between OrgA and OrgB.
OrgA is the data controller, and OrgB is a processor.
The DSA has two purposes: fraud detection and reporting.
OrgA uses the data categories contact, health, and biometric for these purposes.
The DSA has a governance plan, an incident response plan, and an exit plan.
The data is stored for 6 months and then deleted.
OrgA has a transparency arrangement, and OrgB has a rights handling procedure.
The DSA is assessed as high-risk, and the data is transferred to ZA with pseudonymisation and key separation as safeguards.
The DSA has a population of employees, and OrgA is responsible for handling rights requests.
The DSA has a review note justifying the use of data categories contact, health, and biometric.
