This Data Sharing Agreement (DSA) is a contract between OrgB and OrgE.
OrgB is the Data Controller, responsible for collecting and processing personal data.
OrgE is a Processor, authorized to process the data on behalf of OrgB.

The DSA has two purposes: fraud detection and research.
It covers the processing of health and biometric data, subject to legal bases of contract and public interest.
The data is stored for 12 months, after which it is securely deleted.

OrgB has an international transfer plan to Japan, using Binding Corporate Rules and pseudonymization as safeguards.
OrgB has a rights handling plan for the right to erasure, with OrgB as the responsible party and a procedure URL.
OrgB has a transparency arrangement with direct notice to the issuer, OrgB.
OrgB has an assessment plan for DPIA with medium risk and a reference to ICO-2021-§3.2.
OrgB has a governance plan with review frequency of 6 months, metric of AuditFindings<=2, and audit right on-site yearly.
OrgE has an incident response plan with notification deadline of 72 hours and contact point mailto:sec@example.org.
OrgE has an exit plan with exit trigger contract_end, data disposition SecureDelete, and post-termination obligation no redisclosure.
The population covered by the DSA is general.
