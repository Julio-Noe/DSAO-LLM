The following DSA is a research and fraud-detection agreement between OrgC (data controller) and OrgE (recipient).

The agreement includes the following processing activities:
- Research: The data controller processes personal data for research purposes, based on legitimate interest. The data categories used are financial and biometric.
- Fraud detection: The data controller processes personal data for fraud detection purposes, based on legitimate interest. The data category used is health.

The agreement includes the following dataset:
- Dataset 1: A shared dataset that conforms to a standard. The data is stored for 6 months and will be disposed of by anonymization.

The agreement includes the following international transfer:
- Destination country: Australia
- Transfer mechanism: SCCs
- Safeguards: Pseudonymization and access control

The agreement includes the following rights handling:
- Right of access: The data controller is responsible for handling requests for access to personal data. The procedure URL is https://example.org/dsa-rights.

The agreement includes the following transparency arrangement:
- Notice type: Indirect
- Issuer: OrgC
- URL: https://example.org/dsa-notice

The agreement includes the following assessment:
- Assessment type: DPIA
- Outcome: Medium
- References: https://example.org/dpia.pdf

The agreement includes the following governance plan:
- Review frequency: Every 6 months
- Metric: Audit findings <= 2
- Audit right: Third-party audit

The agreement includes the following incident response plan:
- Notification deadline: 72 hours
- Contact point: mailto:sec@example.org

The agreement includes the following exit plan:
- Exit trigger: Contract end

- Employees
