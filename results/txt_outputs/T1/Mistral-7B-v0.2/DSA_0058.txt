The following Data Sharing Agreement (DSA) is between OrgC and OrgE.

OrgC is the Data Controller, responsible for processing personal data according to the DSA. OrgE is the Recipient, which will receive and process personal data according to the DSA.

The DSA has two purposes: service provision and reporting. The processing activities are:
- Service Provision: This activity is subject to the legal basis of public interest. The data categories used are contact and location.
- Reporting: This activity is subject to the legal basis of legal obligation. The data category used is contact.

The DSA includes a governance plan, an incident response plan, and an exit plan. The governance plan specifies that the agreement will be reviewed every 6 months, and that audits will be conducted on-site annually. The incident response plan specifies that notifications must be made within 72 hours of an incident, and that the contact point is sec@example.org. The exit plan specifies that data will be disposed of using the SecureDelete method, and that no contact will be allowed after the exit.

The DSA includes a population of general individuals, and rights handling for the right to erasure. The transparency arrangement is a layered notice issued by OrgC, with the URL <https>.
